# Feature Specification: Photo albums

**Feature Branch**: `001-photo-albums`

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - Group by date (Priority: P1)
Users lose track of photos in one long list.
**Acceptance**: Given 3 photos from 2 days,
when I open Albums, then I see 2 albums.

### User Story 2 - Move photos (Priority: P2)
Move a photo into another album.

## Requirements
- **FR-001**: Group photos by capture date
- **FR-002**: Move a photo between albums
